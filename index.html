PROCESS

How it works.

A systematic, hands-off pipeline that transforms long-form catalogues into omnipresent distribution.

Step 01

Understand your content and audience.

We audit your full archive of podcasts, keynotes, and long-form video. We analyze your core thesis, audience demographics, and high-resonance moments to define your distribution blueprint.

Step 02

Build your dedicated channels.

We establish, brand, and configure independent distribution channels tailored to specific viewer verticals across platforms, establishing algorithmic trust from day one.

Step 03

Create and distribute short-form daily.

Our editorial team transforms your long-form footage into short-form assets built to travel. We schedule, optimize, and publish them every single day across your dedicated channels.

Step 04

Report, refine, scale.

We track retention curves, watch-time telemetry, and audience migration weekly. We double down on breakout formats and scale channel volume as momentum compounds.
